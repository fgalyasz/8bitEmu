# Decision log: Play, save, and scale

## 2026-10-04 — Ear pulses, not a poked block

- **Decision:** TAP and TZX become ear pulses on port `0xFE` bit 6. The ROM loader and a sampler both hear the same tape.
- **Source:** Product request, 2026-10-04. A memory poke would skip turbo and direct-recording loaders.
- **Landed in:** FR-1.

## 2026-10-04 — Nearest neighbor at fractional sizes

- **Decision:** 125%, 150%, 175%, 200%, and free fit sample the source with integer division. The shader does not blend neighbors.
- **Source:** Product request, 2026-10-04. The present pipe stays free of bilinear filtering.
- **Landed in:** FR-5.

## 2026-10-04 — SNA plus standard-speed MIC

- **Decision:** F11 writes an SNA. Port `0xFE` bit 3 is decoded as a standard-speed TAP. That is the BASIC `SAVE` path.
- **Source:** Product request, 2026-10-04.
- **Landed in:** FR-3.

## 2026-10-04 — Kempston is port 0x1F only

- **Decision:** Only the low byte `0x1F` reads the stick. Idle is `0x00`. Arrows and Right Alt are the host keys.
- **Source:** Product request, 2026-10-04. A wider port decode would hide the `0xFF` read used by other ports.
- **Landed in:** FR-4.

## 2026-10-04 — Accuracy stays deferred

- **Decision:** Contention, the floating bus, and flag bits 3 and 5 are not in this increment.
- **Source:** Product request, 2026-10-04. They are not the publish gate.
- **Landed in:** Non-goals.
