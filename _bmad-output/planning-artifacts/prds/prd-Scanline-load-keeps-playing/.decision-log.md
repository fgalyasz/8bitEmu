# Decision log — Keep the tape playing through a load

Created: 2026-10-04
Status: draft

## 2026-10-04 — Fast path

- **Decision:** Latch playback after the first frame with at least 64 port `0xFE` reads, and leave it latched through the ROM's leader wait. The pause after a block is the next edge at the current level, not a forced low. A forced low merged with a low final pulse, so the checksum bit was read as one instead of zero.
- **Source:** Ferenc said loading still does not work. A 48K ROM probe typed `LOAD ""`, then the leader wait stopped the tape, and the merged pause flipped the header checksum.
