## 2026-10-04 — Fast path

- **Decision:** The next increment is the 48K display file driven by our own Z80 program. The Spectrum ROM is not bundled and is not required to see a picture.
- **Source:** Foundation non-goals, product discussion 2026-10-04.
- **Landed in:** Vision, FR-1, non-goals.

- **Decision:** This Z80 is the unprefixed subset the picture needs. Unknown opcodes fail loudly. Cycle-accurate contention waits.
- **Source:** Scope of one hobby increment.
- **Landed in:** FR-3, non-goals.

- **Decision:** `HALT` ends a frame. The next source frame resumes after it, which is the interrupt wake without a ROM handler.
- **Source:** No ROM and no IM 1 jump into `0x0038`.
- **Landed in:** FR-3.

- **Decision:** Flag bits 3 and 5 stay clear. The tested bits are `S Z H PV N C`.
- **Source:** Those bits are not part of the picture.
- **Landed in:** FR-3.
